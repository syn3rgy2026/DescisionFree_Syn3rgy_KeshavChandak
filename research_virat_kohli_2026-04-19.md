# Comprehensive Research Report: Virat Kohli
*Date: 2026-04-19*

## Executive Summary
Virat Kohli is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket. Known for his exceptional batting skills, aggressive leadership, and rigorous fitness standards, Kohli has left an indelible mark on the sport across all three international formats.

## Early Life and Career Rise
Born on November 5, 1988, in New Delhi, India, Virat Kohli (nicknamed 'Cheeku') began his rise to prominence as a right-handed top-order batter. He first gained international attention by leading India to victory in the 2008 ICC U19 World Cup in Malaysia. His international debut followed shortly after in ODIs (2008), T20Is (2010), and Tests (2011). He has been a staple for Delhi and the Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) since their inception/early years.

## Career Statistics and Records
Kohli's statistical record is among the most imposing in cricket history:
- **Test Cricket**: 123 matches, 9,230 runs (avg 46.85) with 30 centuries, including a record 7 double centuries for India.
- **ODI Cricket**: 311 matches, 14,797 runs (avg 58.72) with 54 centuries, holding the record for the most ODI hundreds.
- **T20I Cricket**: 125 matches, 4,188 runs (avg 48.69).

He has won the ICC World Cup (2011), Champions Trophy (2013, 2025), and T20 World Cup (2024), and has been named Player of the Tournament at three separate global events (2014, 2016, 2023).

## Captaincy and Leadership
As the former all-format captain of the Indian National Team, Kohli is credited with transforming India's approach to fast bowling and athletic fitness. His tenure was marked by a passionate, aggressive leadership style and historic successes in overseas Test series, establishing India as a dominant force in red-ball cricket globally.

## Brand Influence and Personal Life
Off the field, Virat Kohli is a global icon. He is one of the most followed athletes in the world on Instagram and maintains an extensive portfolio of high-profile brand endorsements. He married actress Anushka Sharma in 2017 and is frequently recognized for his influence on global sports fitness and professionalism.

## Key Takeaways
- Record-breaking ODI centurion.
- Transformed India's fitness and pace-bowling culture.
- Former all-format captain with historic overseas wins.
- Global brand ambassador for sport and fitness.
