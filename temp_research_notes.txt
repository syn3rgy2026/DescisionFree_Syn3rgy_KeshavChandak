


<b>Sub-topic:</b> Physical characteristics and atmospheric composition / Potential for Life / Evil Twin
<b>Source:</b> https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/

- Physicals: Second planet from Sun, Earth's closest planetary neighbor. Third brightest object in the sky after Sun and Moon. Spins slowly in the opposite direction from most planets.
- Atmosphere: Thick atmosphere traps heat via a runaway greenhouse effect. Surface temperatures reach ~872°F (467°C), hot enough to melt lead. Surface pressure is ~93 times that of Earth's sea level. Clouds are composed of corrosive acid.
- Comparison: Similar in structure and size to Earth, often called "Earth's evil twin".
- Potential for Life: At ~50 km altitude, temperatures (30-70°C) and pressure are similar to Earth's surface, potentially accommodating extremophile microbes. 
- Atmospheric Anomalies: Persistent dark streaks at cloud tops absorb UV radiation; theories include fine particles, ice crystals, iron chloride, or microbial life.
- Distance: Average distance from Sun is 67 million miles (108 million km) or 0.72 AU. Sunlight takes about 6 minutes to reach it.
- Size: Diameter at equator is approximately 7,521 miles (12,104 km).
